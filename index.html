Theoretical & Computational Physics

I am currently pursuing a BSc in Theoretical Physics at the University of Science, VNU-HCM.

My current research interests include electronic-structure theory, quantum many-body physics, computational quantum chemistry and condensed matter physics.

I am currently conducting research at QuantumLab under the supervision of Dr. Lan Nguyen Tran. My current research focuses on developing a restricted open-shell formulation of the group's One-Body Møller–Plesset second-order perturbation theory (OBMP2), referred to as RO-OBMP2.

Electronic Structure Theory

Current topics include the ROHF reference, perturbative amplitudes, Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff expansions, effective one-body Hamiltonians, spin properties, and numerical benchmarks.
